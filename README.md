### CHEM-000329: Distinguishing dissolution-driven and rearrangement-driven cement creep
- **Subfield:** Round 2
- **Open:** Separate stress-assisted dissolution/reprecipitation, hydrate sliding and continuing hydration contributions to creep, predicting their humidity and composition dependence.
- **Status:** The microscopic origin remains debated. A 2025 study found continued fly-ash reaction increasing creep and foil-like C-A-S-H in slag blends inhibiting it; quantitative mechanism separation remains open.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Links hydrate chemistry to long-term structural deformation.
- **Key works:** J. Wang et al. (2025), *Does the hydration process of supplementary cementitious materials affect the aging creep of blended cement paste?*, [DOI:10.1016/j.cemconres.2025.107826](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cemconres.2025.107826).
- **Related:** Solid mechanics: constitutive laws for aging porous materials.
